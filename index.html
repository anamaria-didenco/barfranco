Bar Franco · 03 · Where it comes from

Three generations, one way of treating people.

Everything I know about hospitality, I learned at my grandmother’s table. The Franco script above the door is drawn from my mother’s signature — her hand, kept. The dachshund is Humphrey. I just gave it all an Italian accent.

— Ana-Maria
