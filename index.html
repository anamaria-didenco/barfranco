Not a bar. A world.

The soul of Bar Franco is Moldovan, even if the accent is Italian. Everything we know about hospitality we learned at a grandmother’s table, in a country with little to spare and everything to give: no empty glasses, no one unfed, every stranger welcomed like a long-lost friend. That table came all the way from Moldova; the name above the door is written in my mother’s own hand.

Franco means frank. No pretending, just house-made pasta and a room that wants you in it.
