About Bar Franco

We don't perform. We host. An Italian restaurant and Negroni bar on two levels in central Christchurch.

An Italian restaurant and cocktail bar on two levels at The Crossing, in the middle of Christchurch. No ceremony, no one reciting the specials at you — just a room that's pleased you came.

Come up for dinner and the house-made pasta, take the long table for private events and functions in Christchurch, or stay downstairs for a Negroni. Ti aspettiamo — we're waiting for you.

Two levels. One world.

Level 2 · The Restaurant

We cook like someone's coming home.

Pasta is rolled, cut and dressed a few steps from your table — pici with cacio e pepe, tagliatelle under a slow pork ragù, gnocchi that barely holds together. The open kitchen means you watch it happen.

The rest of the menu moves with the season: burrata while the stone fruit lasts, whatever the market boat brought in, greens over the coals. Order too much. That's rather the point of a long Italian dinner.

Level 1 · The Negroni Bar

The night is yours.

Downstairs runs on the oldest Italian habit there is: a bitter drink, something salty, and nowhere in particular to be. Negronis stirred to order, sbagliati when the mood is lighter, spritzes arriving by the tray.

It starts at four with one quiet drink after work. By nine the room has found its volume and nobody is checking the time.
