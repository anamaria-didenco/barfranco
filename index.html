aperitivo

Every evening has a beginning, and ours is aperitivo: cinque a sette, from five to seven on Level 1. A spritz, a Negroni stirred to order, something salty to nibble, and the night getting started, just as it should be. Walk-ins are always welcome at the bar.

I’m Ana-Maria, and Bar Franco is the room I didn’t want to leave: a Negroni bar downstairs, a restaurant upstairs, and a long table in the middle of the city. Nobody’s glass is ever empty, everyone is fed, and every guest is a long-lost friend we’ve been waiting for.
