The terms

  1. Promoter. Bar Franco Limited, 166 Cashel Street, The Crossing, Christchurch 8011.

  2. Who. The individual (18+) named as booking contact on a confirmed private event at Bar Franco. The person, not the company.

  3. How. Entry is automatic for every private event confirmed with deposit paid between 15 October and 11.59pm 20 November 2026, for an event held between 15 October and 31 December 2026. No code, nothing to claim. One entry per confirmed booking.

  4. The draw. One name drawn at random at Bar Franco, Level 1, at 6.00pm on Monday 23 November 2026. All entrants are invited; attendance is not required to win. The winner is contacted by phone and email the same evening. No reply within 48 hours and a new name is drawn.

  5. The prize. Two Pretty Face Package tickets to Robbie Williams, One New Zealand Stadium, Christchurch, Saturday 28 November 2026 — front GA standing, early entry — and a $200 gift card for The Crossing. Total value $1,237.57. No cash alternative. The gift card is subject to The Crossing’s own terms and expiry. Travel and everything else are the winner’s own.

  6. Cancellations. A cancelled booking voids the entry; if already drawn, a new name is drawn. If the concert is cancelled or postponed, Bar Franco passes on whatever the ticket seller provides and has no further liability.

  7. Excluded. Bar Franco staff and their immediate families.

  8. Privacy. Names and contact details are used only to run this promotion and the booking. The winner’s first name may appear on Bar Franco’s social media.

  9. The usual. Bar Franco’s decision is final. New Zealand law applies.

This promotion is in no way sponsored, endorsed, administered by or associated with Instagram, Meta, Ticketmaster or the concert promoter.
