Before you ask

The usual questions

How early should we book a work Christmas party?

December dates go first — the good Fridays are usually gone by October, and the fortnight before Christmas fills fastest. Midweek December nights are easier to get and often the better night out. Send us the date you have in mind and we'll tell you honestly whether it's still open.

How many of us can you take?

Up to 240 across both levels standing, or 165 seated. Downstairs takes 120 standing or 75 seated; upstairs takes 120 standing or 90 seated.

Is there a venue hire fee?

No. There's a minimum spend on the food and drinks instead, which depends on the date and the space — so your budget goes into the night, not the room.

How much is a Christmas party per person?

The shared Christmas menu is $80 a head, canapés with the shared menu together are $110 a head, and a standing grazing table is $40 a head. Canapés on their own start at $9 a person, and a drinks package can be added for $45 a head.

Can we have the venue to ourselves?

Yes — either level on its own, or both levels and both bars with the doors closed to everyone else.

Do you handle dietaries and allergies?

Every menu has a vegetarian and a gluten-free line built in. Vegan, dairy-free and most allergies are handled in the planning, not on the night. See our menus.

Where are you, and where does everyone park?

We're in The Crossing at 166 Cashel Street, in the middle of the CBD next to H&M. The Crossing car park is in the same block and central bus and tram stops are minutes away, so everyone can get there and get home easily.
