How booking works
Tell us your date, numbers and the kind of night you want. We send a tailored proposal and the events pack. A deposit from 500 secures the date, taken off your drinks bill. We confirm the run of the night, and you turn up and enjoy it.
Minimum spend
Friday and Saturday nights carry a food and drink minimum, set by the space: from 4,000 for the bar. Midweek there’s usually none. December Fridays book out months ahead.
On the night
Drinks run on consumption and we keep you posted on the spend, so there are no surprises on the invoice. Microphones for speeches, a projector and screen, and room for a band or a DJ. Last drinks 11:30pm.
Dietaries and custom menus
Every dietary handled, and custom food and drink menus on request. Final numbers and dietaries are due five days before.
Getting here
Upstairs at 166 Cashel Street, The Crossing. Take the lift near H&M and Coffee Culture; The Crossing carpark is right there. We’re closed from 23 December to 5 January.
