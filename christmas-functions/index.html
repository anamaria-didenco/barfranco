Bar Franco · 04 · Good to know

Questions

How early should we book?+−

As early as you can — December Fridays and Saturdays go months ahead. Midweek dates are great value and easier to get.

How many people can you host?+−

Up to 240 across both levels, around 165 seated. Single-level and smaller group bookings are welcome too.

Is there a venue hire fee?+−

No. We work to a food and beverage minimum spend, tailored to your group, date and space.

How much per person?+−

Shared set menus from 80pp, or 110pp with canapés on arrival. Standing: canapés from 9 each, grazing tables from 40pp.

Can you cater dietaries?+−

Vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free and most allergies — just tell us when you confirm numbers.
