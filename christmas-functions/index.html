Good to know

Christmas function FAQs

How early should we book our work Christmas party?

As early as you can — prime December Friday and Saturday dates book out first, often months ahead. Midweek December dates are great value and easier to grab. Enquire now to lock in your preferred date.

How many people can you host?

Up to 240 across both levels — around 165 seated, or the whole venue for a standing cocktail function. Single-level and smaller group bookings are very welcome too.

Is there a venue hire fee?

No venue hire fee. We work to a food and beverage minimum spend instead, tailored to your group, date and space.

How much is a Christmas party per person?

Shared set menus start at 80 per person, with a 110 per person option that adds canapés on arrival. For standing functions, canapés are 9 each (4–6 per person recommended) and grazing tables start at 40 per person. No venue hire fee — we work to a food and beverage minimum spend instead.

Can you cater dietaries?

Absolutely — vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free and most allergies are no problem. Just let us know when you confirm numbers.
