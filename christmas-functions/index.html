04 · How it works

Enquire now

One email. Sorted.

One person looks after you, from your first email to the last drink.

How do I book?

Send your date and numbers. Within one business day we send a proposal and the events pack. A deposit from $500 holds the date, and comes off your drinks bill.

What will it cost?

Shared menus from $80pp (grazing table from $40pp), drinks on consumption, no venue hire fee. Friday and Saturday carry a minimum spend, from $4,000 for the bar; midweek, usually none. We keep you posted on the spend on the night, so the invoice holds no surprises.

Do you do Christmas lunch?

Yes. Lunch or dinner, on either level. Tell us the day and the time in your enquiry.

What’s included on the night?

Your host, microphones for speeches, a projector and screen, and room for a band or a DJ. Last drinks 11:30pm.

Can you handle dietaries?

Every one. Custom food and drink menus on request; final numbers and dietaries five days before.

How do guests get there?

Upstairs at 166 Cashel Street, The Crossing, in the CBD. Lift near H&M and KOOKAÏ; the carpark is right there. Closed 24 December to 5 January.
