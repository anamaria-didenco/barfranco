Bar Franco04Pasta FAQs

Good to know

Pasta FAQs

Is the pasta made in-house?

It is — we roll our pasta fresh in the open kitchen upstairs. You can watch it happen.

Do you have gluten-free pasta?

Yes — tell us when you book or order and the kitchen will look after you.

Can I see the menu before I visit?

Of course — the full food and drinks menu is on our Menus page.

What time does the kitchen open?

Daily from 5pm, till late. The bar downstairs opens from 4pm.
