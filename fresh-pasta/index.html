02 · The kitchen

The extruder sits in the open kitchen, so the pasta is made in view: dough in one end, pasta out the other, into the pan and onto the pass. No back room, no mystery.

Pici come out thick and chewy for cacio e pepe, cavatelli small enough to hold the clams, tagliatelle wide enough to carry the ragù. Each shape is made for its sauce.

Level 2 is built around the kitchen, with a long table for the bigger nights. Sit where you can see it all, or sit back with a glass and let the room do the work. More on the restaurant.

03 · How to do it

Spritz first. Pasta second.

Start downstairs on Level 1 with a spritz or a Negroni at the bar, then come up for dinner, and back down for a nightcap if the night allows.

Can’t decide? Trust Franco: the kitchen sends out a run of dishes to share, five courses or seven, and nobody at the table has to choose a thing.
