What aperitivo looks like here

Spritzes

Aperol, Campari, limoncello, Hugo — bright, sparkling, made for an afternoon that drifts into evening.

Negronis

The classic, the Sbagliato, the Cherry — bitter, balanced, very Franco.

Cicchetti

Olives, focaccia, salumi and whatever the kitchen cut that afternoon.

Wine by the glass

Prosecco, an Italian-leaning list, and low- and no-alcohol options for the driver.

Bar Franco04Good to know

Questions

What time is aperitivo?

Cinque a sette runs 5 to 7 nightly; the bar is open daily from 4pm, till late.

Do I need to book?

No — walk in and pull up a stool. For a group, or to stay for dinner, book a table.

What's on the board?

Cicchetti to go with your drink — olives, focaccia, arancini and salumi. Ask what's on tonight.

Where is the bar?

Downstairs at Franco, in The Crossing at 166 Cashel Street.
