Good to know

Corporate event FAQs

How many guests can you host?

Up to 240 across both levels standing, or around 165 seated. A single level suits team dinners and client evenings; take both for the whole company.

Is there a venue hire fee?

No venue hire fee — we work to a food and beverage minimum spend instead, tailored to your event, date and space.

Do you offer set menus and dietary options?

Yes — shared set menus from 80 per person, canapé and grazing options for standing events, and full dietary coverage. See our menus.

How easy is it for staff and clients to get there?

Very — we're in The Crossing at 166 Cashel Street, in the middle of the CBD, next to H&M. The Crossing car park is in the same block and central bus and tram stops are minutes away.

How far ahead should we book?

For December and Friday nights, as early as you can — Christmas dates go first. Midweek dates are often available at shorter notice.
