Bar Franco · 04 · Good to know

What planners ask

How many guests can you host?

Up to 240 standing across both floors, or around 165 seated. One floor suits a team or client dinner.

Is there a venue hire fee?

No. A food and drink minimum spend instead: usually none Sunday to Thursday; Fridays and Saturdays from 4,000 for the bar, 7,500 for the Restaurant, 15,000 for the whole building.

How quickly will I hear back?

Within one business day, with availability, a proposal and the events pack.

Is there AV for speeches and presentations?

Microphones, a projector and screen, and room for a band or a DJ.

How does billing work?

A deposit from 500 holds the date and comes off your drinks bill. Drinks run on consumption, and we keep you posted on the spend on the night.

Do you offer set menus and dietaries?

Shared set menus from 80pp, canapés and grazing tables, and every dietary handled.

How easy is it to get there?

The Crossing, 166 Cashel Street, in the CBD. Lift near H&M; the carpark is in the same block, buses and tram minutes away.

How far ahead should we book?

December and Friday nights: as early as you can. Midweek: often at short notice.

The work do, sorted

December and Friday nights go first — enquire early.
