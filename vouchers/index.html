Voucher questions

How do I buy a gift voucher?+−

Buy online and it's emailed instantly, or pick one up in person next time you're in.

What can a voucher be used for?+−

Anything at Franco — dinner upstairs, drinks at the bar, a long-table celebration. Treat it like cash toward the night.

Can I send one as a gift?+−

Yes — have it emailed straight to the lucky recipient, or grab a physical one in person.

Do vouchers expire?+−

Expiry and full terms are shown at checkout, so you'll know exactly what you're giving.

Can I use it for a function?+−

Absolutely — put it toward a group booking or event. Just mention it when you get in touch.
