Bar Franco · 04 · Good to know

Questions

Is there a private dining area?

Yes, at the far end of the upstairs Restaurant. Smaller groups can also take the bar; bigger ones, a level or the whole building.

Do we need to hire a level for a big birthday?

No. For ten or more we set you up with the events team and a shared menu, no hire needed.

Is there a venue hire fee?

No. A food and drink minimum spend instead: usually none Sunday to Thursday; Fridays and Saturdays from 4,000 for the bar, 7,500 for the Restaurant, 15,000 for the whole building.

How much is a group menu?

Shared set menus from 80pp, grazing tables from 40pp, canapés from 9 each. Vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free and most allergies are no problem.

How many can you hold?

Up to 240 standing across both levels, around 165 seated.

How do we find you?

Upstairs at 166 Cashel Street, The Crossing. Take the lift near H&M and KOOKAÏ, or the stairs. The Crossing carpark is right there.
