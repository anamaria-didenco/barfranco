Booking questions

Do you take walk-ins?+−

Always — the bar downstairs keeps stools for walk-ins. For a dinner table, especially Thursday to Saturday or for a group, booking ahead is the safe bet.

How do I book a table?+−

Use the live availability on this page, or call 021 221 1307. For ten or more, or the whole venue, the functions team will tailor the night.

Can you cater dietary requirements?+−

Vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free and most allergies are no trouble. Note it in your booking or tell your server.

What are your opening hours?+−

Open daily — the bar from 4pm and the kitchen from 5pm, till late.

Is there parking nearby?+−

The Crossing car park is in the same block, with plenty of central-city parking close by.

Planning something bigger?

Private dining, cocktail functions and the whole building · up to 240 guests
