Before you ask

The usual questions

Is there a venue hire fee?

No. There's a minimum spend on food and drinks instead, set by the date and the space — so the budget goes into the night, not the room.

How many of us fit on each level?

The Negroni Bar on Level 1 takes 120 standing or 75 seated. The restaurant on Level 2 takes 120 standing or 90 seated. Take both and it's up to 240 standing, or 165 seated.

Can we have the building to ourselves?

Yes — either level exclusively on its own, or both levels and both bars with the doors closed to everyone else.

What do the menus cost?

A standing grazing table is $40 a head; the shared seated menu is $80; canapés with the shared menu together are $110. Canapés on their own start at $9 a person, and a drinks package can be added at $45 a head. See our menus.

Dietaries, allergies, the fussy one?

Every menu has a vegetarian and a gluten-free line built in. Vegan, dairy-free and most allergies are handled in the planning rather than on the night — tell us when you book and it's simply taken care of.

Screens, microphones, a playlist?

There's a screen and sound for speeches and slides, and you're welcome to bring your own playlist. Tell us when the speeches are and the room gets quiet at the right moment.

Deposit, invoicing and cancellation?

We'll hold a date while you check with everyone. A deposit confirms it, and the balance is invoiced after the night — 20th-of-the-month terms if that suits your finance team better. Plans change, so talk to us early and we'll work it out.

Parking, and how close is the stadium?

We're in The Crossing at 166 Cashel Street, next to H&M. The Crossing car park is in the same block, central bus and tram stops are minutes away, and One NZ Stadium is about five minutes on foot.
