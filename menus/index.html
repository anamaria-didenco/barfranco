Bar Franco · 03 · Good to know

Menu questions

Do you cater for dietary requirements?

Absolutely — vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free and most allergies are well looked after. Just let us know when you book or order.

Is the pasta made in-house?

It is — rolled fresh in the open kitchen upstairs. You can watch it happen.

Can I see the menu before I visit?

Yes — the full food and drinks menus are right here, with PDFs to print.

Do you have a cocktail and drinks menu?

Negronis, spritzes, classic cocktails and a tidy Italian wine list, led by the Negroni & Spritz Bar downstairs.

Do the menus change?

With the seasons — so there's always something new to try.
