Bar Franco · 04 · Good to know

Questions

How far is it to One NZ Stadium?

About a five-minute stroll from The Crossing on Cashel Street.

When should we eat before an event?

Around two hours before kick-off or doors is the sweet spot. On event nights, book ahead.

Do you take bookings on event nights?

Yes — online or on 021 221 1307. Reserve early; these are our busiest nights.

Can we come back after?

The Negroni & Spritz Bar runs till late — swing back for a nightcap.

Is it good for a group?

Very. Groups across two levels with a shared menu to eat well and quickly.
