05 · Good to know

What planners ask

Is there a venue hire fee?

No. A food and drink minimum spend instead: usually none Sunday to Thursday; Fridays and Saturdays from 4,000 for the bar, 7,500 for the Restaurant, 15,000 for the whole building.

How quickly will I hear back?

Within one business day, with a proposal and the events pack.

Is there AV for speeches?

Microphones, a projector and screen, and room for a band or a DJ.

Can you cater dietaries?

Vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free and most allergies.

How far ahead should I book?

Fridays, Saturdays and December: as early as you can. Midweek: often at short notice.

Can I book a large group without hiring a level?

Yes. From ten guests, with a shared menu.
