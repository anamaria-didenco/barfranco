Bar Franco · 05 · Good to know

Function questions

How many guests can you hold?

Up to 240 across both levels — around 165 seated, or the whole venue for a standing cocktail function. Single-level hire is available too.

Is there a venue hire fee?

No. We work to a food and beverage minimum spend instead, tailored to your event, date and space.

Can you cater dietaries?

Vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free and most allergies — just let us know when you confirm.

How far ahead should I book?

For Friday and Saturday nights and December, as early as you can. Midweek dates are often free at shorter notice.

Can I book a large group without hiring a level?

Yes — for ten or more we'll set you up with the events team and a shared menu, no hire needed.

Do you offer set menus for groups?

Shared Italian set menus built around your group, budget and dietaries, from antipasti to dolci.

Can you host a private birthday dinner?

One of our favourite things. Tell us the occasion and we'll make it special.

Booking the work do?

December dates go first — lock yours in.
