How it works

Seven steps, from first email to last drinks

05From first email to last drinks

How it works

  1. 01

    Tell us about the night

    The date, how many, seated or standing, and a rough budget. If it’s a milestone, say so.

  2. 02

    We call, then propose

    A quick chat first. Then the right space, your minimum spend and menu ideas in writing, with your date pencilled in.

  3. 03

    Come in

    Any day from 3:30pm, for a drink on us and a walk through both levels.

  4. 04

    Lock it in

    A deposit secures the date. First to confirm gets it.

  5. 05

    Shape the details

    A few weeks out, one simple checklist: food, drinks, numbers, dietaries, timings, music, AV, décor and cake.

  6. 06

    Sign off your runsheet

    Final numbers and dietaries are due five days out, and we invoice your food then.

  7. 07

    We host. You stay.

    From the first spritz to last drinks, our team runs the night so you can be in the room.

Questions

06Good to know

What planners ask

Is there a venue hire fee?

No. A food and drink minimum spend instead: usually none Sunday to Thursday; Fridays and Saturdays from 4,000 for the bar, 7,500 for the Restaurant, 15,000 for the whole building.

How quickly will I hear back?

Within one business day, with a proposal and the events pack.

Is there AV for speeches?

Microphones, a projector and screen, and room for a band or a DJ.

Can you cater dietaries?

Vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free and most allergies.

How far ahead should I book?

Fridays, Saturdays and December: as early as you can. Midweek: often at short notice.

Can I book a large group without hiring a level?

Yes. From ten guests, with a shared menu.
