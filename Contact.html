Visiting Franco

Where is Bar Franco?

In The Crossing at 166 Cashel Street, Christchurch Central 8011 — the heart of the CBD, by H&M on the laneway side.

What are your opening hours?

Open daily — the bar from 4pm and the kitchen from 5pm, both till late.

Is there parking nearby?

The Crossing car park is in the same block, plus plenty of central-city parking a short walk away.

How far is it from One NZ Stadium?

A short walk — a handy table before the game or a concert at Te Kaha.

How do I get in touch about an event?

Email anamaria@barfranco.nz or call 021 221 1307 with your date and numbers.
