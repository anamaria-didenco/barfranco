Good to know

Function FAQs

How many guests can you hold?

Up to 240 across both levels — around 165 seated, or the whole venue for a standing cocktail function. Single-level hire is also available.

Is there a venue hire fee?

No venue hire fee. We work to a food and beverage minimum spend instead, tailored to your event, date and space.

Can you cater dietaries?

Absolutely. Our kitchen handles vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free and most allergies — just let us know when you confirm.

How far ahead should I book?

For peak dates (Friday/Saturday nights and December) we recommend enquiring as early as possible. Midweek dates are often available at shorter notice.

Can I book a large group without hiring the whole venue?

Yes — group bookings and long tables are very welcome. For around 10 or more we'll set you up with our events team and a shared menu, without needing to hire a whole level.

Do you offer set menus for groups?

We do — shared Italian set menus built around your group, budget and dietaries, from antipasti to dolci. See our menus.

Can you host a private dinner for a birthday or occasion?

Absolutely — private dining for birthdays, engagements, anniversaries and milestones is one of our favourite things. Tell us the occasion and we'll make it special.
