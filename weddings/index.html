Bar Franco · 04 · Good to know

Questions

Can we have the ceremony and reception here?

Yes — vows on one level, celebrate on the other, all under one roof.

How many guests?

Up to 240 standing across both levels, around 165 seated. One level suits intimate weddings.

Is there a venue hire fee?

No. A food and drink minimum spend instead: usually none Sunday to Thursday; Fridays and Saturdays from 4,000 for the bar, 7,500 for the Restaurant, 15,000 for the whole building.

Can you cater dietaries?

Vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free and most allergies, with seated menus from 80pp.

When should we enquire?

As early as you can — prime weekends book out well ahead.
